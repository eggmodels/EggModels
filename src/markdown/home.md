# Welcome to EggModels
#### Mathew Durie, Philip Weaver, Jacques Morris, Eugenio Garza Garcia, Sebastian Garza Garcia

___

At EggModels, we crack open prediction markets with a blend of classical statical modeling blended with modern machine-learning techniques and live-data monitoring to detect inefficiencies and generate data-driven insights we can turn into learnable real-world trades. We began by modeling tennis and American football markets; now we’re expanding into economics and politics to test how classical understanding of public markets holds up in this new arena.
